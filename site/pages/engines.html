Newton on CPU. Every preset of the four generators is opened in Newton 1.8.0 with its XPBD solver, from its Newton layer: the file with what Newton cannot guess from standard UsdPhysics (contact gaps, limits on a cable's free joint axes, battery holders as articulations, cloth in the AOUSD proposal's schemas). Mass against the pack; grains and parts settle, cloth lands at rest, a hung cable bends. Cable and cloth are not compared with MuJoCo yet.

Fine grains at any scale. Sand and regolith are simulated with lengths and gravity scaled by the same factor, which leaves the motion unchanged and keeps engines away from their absolute tolerances. Each README says how.
